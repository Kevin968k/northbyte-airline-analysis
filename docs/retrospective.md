# Competition retrospective

**Result:** semi-finalist. **Team:** A Special Team. **Date:** October 2026.

The questions below are Kevin's recollection of the judging discussion, not verbatim quotes or an official scoring explanation.

| Judge question | Planned improvement |
|---|---|
| What supports the 40%, 60% and 80% assumptions? | Estimate avoidable costs by category and time horizon using contracts, rosters and operating records |
| How confident are you in the proposed outcome? | Separate historical reconciliation, sensitivity robustness and predictive uncertainty; do not invent a success probability |
| How would leadership implement the plan? | Specify accountable owners, pilot flights, dates, budgets, service criteria, evaluation baseline and stop/scale gates |
| How could losses be reduced further? | Investigate time/season/direction-specific frequencies, capacity matching, cost mechanisms, pricing, refunds and gate terms |

## Completed in the competition

Operating-cost analysis, integrated fiscal/network comparisons, cost-category decomposition, conditional frequency scenarios and a proposed 90-day action plan.

## Future work

- Match booking revenue to flown services.
- Validate category-specific avoidability across 30-, 90- and 365-day horizons.
- Include implementation costs, revenue recapture and network effects without double counting.
- Choose specific pilot flights under schedule, crew and connectivity constraints.
- Evaluate outcomes against an appropriate seasonal baseline and comparable services where feasible.

These are research directions, not completed results or additional quantified savings. The initial scenario did not close the full annual shortfall. The questions alone do not establish why the team did not advance.
